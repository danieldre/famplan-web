Famplan

Where your child sleeps tonight, and everything that goes with it

Famplan is one place for every adult around a child: parents, partners, carers, grandparents. Across one home, two, or more.

  • The nights, at a glance. A custody schedule each home agrees to, coloured by home, never by person.
  • Who picks up, and what travels. Pickups, handovers and the bag that goes with them.
  • Tasks, lists and shared costs. Split the way your family splits them, in your own currency.
  • Each person sees what’s theirs to see. Private, one home, or the whole family: you choose for every item.

In a closed test. Famplan isn’t in the App Store or Google Play yet. If someone invited you, ask them to add you as a tester, then open their invite link again.

No ads. No selling of data. No location tracking. Children don’t have accounts; their details are kept on their parents’ behalf.
