Today

The answer, before anyone asks.

Open Famplan and tonight is already there: which home, who picks up, what travels. From six in the evening it answers for tomorrow.

  • Each home is a place. Its own colour and its own drawing, all the same size. No home is the main one.
  • What travels. The swimming bag, the reading folder, who packed it and when.
  • Swaps without the thread. Ask for a different night. The other home accepts with one tap.

Your year

The whole year, in a minute.

Say how your weeks go, in your own words: “alternate weeks, switching on Fridays”. Famplan draws every night of the year, school holidays included.

  • Each home checks its own nights. Nothing is final until every home has agreed.
  • Tasks and shared costs, too. Split the way your family splits them, in your own currency.

Every family shape

For every adult around a child.

Parents, partners and carers, in one home or several. Nobody is “primary”, and nothing assumes who does what.

  • One home

    Share the load fairly. Tasks have names on them, so nobody has to be the one who remembers.

  • Two homes

    Know which nights are whose, hand over without a long message thread, and settle the school trip.

  • Blended families

    Children who move between several homes, in one combined view. You can be part of more than one family circle.

  • Grandparents and carers

    Just their pickups and where to go. Nothing else unless you share it.

Privacy

Private by default.

Each home keeps a private space. Only what you link to a shared child crosses over.

  • You choose who sees each thing.Only meBoth homesPickup adultsEveryone
  • No location tracking. Famplan never asks where anyone is.
  • No ads, no selling data. Children don’t have accounts; their details are kept on their parents’ behalf.
