Privacy policy

Last updated: [PLACEHOLDER: date this version takes effect]

Famplan helps the adults around a child share one calendar, tasks, lists and costs across one or more homes. That means it holds details about your family, including your children. This page says what we keep, why, who helps us run it, and how you stay in control.

In short: no ads, no selling or sharing of data for advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no location tracking, no analytics on children. Children don’t have accounts. Your family’s data is stored in the EU. A few optional features send text to service providers outside the EU, and we say which below.

2. What we process

About you (an adult with an account)

  • Your email address, used to send you a sign-in code. There are no passwords.
  • The name you choose to show, and the families and homes you belong to, with your role in each (parent, partner or carer).
  • What you add: events, tasks, lists, handover checklists, expenses and receipt photos, swap requests, notes, and child card entries.
  • Settings: notification choices, time zone, which calendars you connect or subscribe to.
  • A push token for each phone you allow notifications on.
  • If you buy Famplan Plus: the store’s transaction identifier and the subscription’s status. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Technical data needed to run the service: sign-in session, the IP address in server logs, and crash reports (see feature by feature).

About children

First name (or whatever name you enter), optional birthday, which home they sleep at on which night, and optional child card entries: shoe and clothes size, school, class, teacher, clubs, and a pickup password. See children’s data.

What we don’t collect

No location, no contacts, no health data, no advertising identifiers, no browsing history. The child card has no health fields on purpose.

3. Children’s data

  • No child accounts. Children don’t sign in. Their details are entered by the adults in their family and kept on those adults’ behalf.
  • Only what a feature needs. A name and, if you want, a birthday. Everything else on the child card is optional, and each entry has its own visibility: everyone in the family, both homes, pickup adults, or only you.
  • No location tracking. An “Arrived” check-in records a time, never a place.
  • No ads, no selling, no profiling. We don’t use children’s data for analytics, advertising or training AI models.
  • Crash reports are filtered on the phone before they’re sent, so they carry no names, dates, messages or screen content.
  • A pickup password is stored separately and is shown only to someone allowed to see it. Each time it’s shown is logged, and the parents can see that log.

Parents and guardians can see, correct and delete their children’s data in the app at any time.

4. Data about other adults

Famplan is shared by design: you may add another adult’s name to an event, a pickup or an expense, or invite them. They see only what the family’s settings let them see. Partners and a home’s carers can be hidden from the other homes. If you are named in someone else’s family and have no account, you can contact us to exercise your rights (section 11).

5. Feature by feature

Your family’s data

Stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Access is enforced in the database itself: each person can read only what their role and each item’s visibility allow.

Sign-in emails

We email you a six-digit code through Resend, from its EU region (Ireland). Resend receives your email address and the code.

Voice and typed notes, shared items (“capture”)

Speech is turned into text on your phone by your phone’s own speech recognition; we never receive audio. When you ask Famplan to understand a note, a shared text, photo or screenshot, or a forwarded school email, we send it to Anthropic’s Claude API, which runs in the United States. With it goes the context needed to route it: first names, home names, which home has the children on which nights, list names, today’s date and, for a note, your own next 14 days of events, tasks, handovers and child card entries you can already see. Photos are shrunk and stripped of their metadata on your phone first.

We don’t store the note or the reply on our servers, and we don’t log their content. Nothing is saved to your family until you confirm it, and anything for another home is a draft until you send it. We count how many reads each family uses per month, without content. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models; it may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring [PLACEHOLDER: confirm Anthropic’s current API retention period and whether zero data retention applies].

First-run sentence

On first launch, before you have an account, you can describe your family in one sentence for Famplan to fill in the set-up. If this is switched on, that sentence goes to Anthropic in the US in the same way, and is neither stored nor logged. To limit abuse we keep a keyed hash of your network address for one day. This feature is currently switched off.

Family email address and school emails

A parent or partner can get a private address to forward school emails to. Mail is received by Mailgun [PLACEHOLDER: Mailgun region, US or EU] and passed to us; we keep only the plain text (without quoted replies or signatures, up to 16,000 characters) in your family’s inbox for 14 days, then delete it. Reading it uses capture, above.

Push notifications

If you allow them, notifications are sent through Expo’s push service (US) to Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). A notification’s text can include first names and home names, for example who picks up on Tuesday. You can turn each kind off in Settings, or turn them all off in your phone’s settings.

Connected Google or Microsoft calendars (optional)

If you connect one, you sign in with Google or Microsoft and allow Famplan to read the calendars you pick and to keep a “Famplan” calendar there with the events you may see. We store the access token encrypted. Imported events are private to you. Disconnect any time in Settings; you can also remove access in your Google or Microsoft account.

Calendar subscriptions and feeds

If you subscribe to a school or club calendar (an ICS link), our server fetches that link regularly. If you create a feed of your own events for another calendar app or a wall display, anyone with that secret link can read it, so treat it like a password; you can revoke it in Settings. Your phone’s own calendar can also be synced on the device, if you allow it.

School holidays

When you pick a region, our server looks up official school holidays from the OpenHolidays API (Germany). Only the region code is sent; nothing about your family.

Famplan Plus purchases

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. They handle payment; we receive and check the transaction with Apple or Google to know which family has Plus and until when.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it can send a report to Sentry [PLACEHOLDER: Sentry region, intended EU (Frankfurt); crash reporting is not yet switched on]. Before it leaves the phone, the report is reduced to technical fields: no user, no screen content, screenshots or view hierarchy, no request bodies or console output, and identifiers, email addresses, dates and database values in error messages are removed.

Usage counts

We count, per week, how many families are active and where new items came from (typed, voice, shared, email, imported). Who was active is kept only for the running week, then reduced to totals. Children are never counted. There are no third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app.

10. Export, deletion, leaving

  • Export: Settings → Your data lets you download your data, your calendar (ICS) and the schedule as a PDF.
  • Delete your account: in the same screen. Your private items, calendar connections, feeds, subscriptions, push tokens, notifications and profile are deleted. Items you shared with the family (events, tasks and expenses others could see, the schedule and swaps) stay with the family, credited to “Former member”, because they are the family’s shared history. If no other adult is left in a family, the whole family is deleted, with its homes and the child profiles that belong to no other family.
  • Leave a family: any adult can leave at any time; a removed member loses access immediately.
  • Subscriptions are cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account; deleting your Famplan account doesn’t cancel them.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, write to us (section 16). We answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work. A parent or guardian can review, correct or delete it in the app or by contacting us. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our database provider. Who can see what is enforced in the database, not only in the app. Calendar access tokens are encrypted separately. Sign-in is by one-time code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

14. This website

getfamplan.app sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Our host logs standard request data such as IP address and time [PLACEHOLDER: host and its log retention].

Invite links keep their code after the “#”, which browsers don’t send to the server. To show who invited you, the invite page asks our database for a preview: the inviter’s name, the family’s name, the children’s first names and, for a co-parent, the proposed nights. The referral page shows only the first name of the person who shared it. Nothing is stored in your browser.
